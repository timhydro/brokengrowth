Our mission

A pathway—not a handout.

Broken Growth Ministries exists to help adults facing the hardest part of reentry and homelessness: the gap between wanting a different life and having the structure, opportunity and support to build one.

Our model brings practical needs together instead of treating them as separate problems. Housing without work can stall. Work without transportation can collapse. Recovery without community can become isolation. We are building one coordinated pathway around the person.

The Broken Growth pathway

Stability first. Then forward motion.

The residential model below is in development and describes how the first Broken Growth facility is intended to operate.

01

Connect before release

When possible, begin the relationship before release so identification, housing, transportation, recovery and employment barriers can be mapped early.

02

30-day stabilization

The first month after admission is designed around documents, benefits, recovery meetings, health needs, transportation and an individualized action plan.

03

Work and skills

After stabilization, participants move into employment, training or active job search based on their plan, abilities and approved exceptions.

04

Build independence

The goal is sustained stability: income, savings, job retention, stronger relationships, practical life skills and a realistic path to independent housing.

My story. Our mission.

I know what it means to need a second chance—because I needed one myself.

I fought through addiction, homelessness and incarceration. There were times when I had little more than the clothes on my back and no clear path forward.

I know what it feels like to be at the bottom, to have people see the worst chapter of your life before they see the person still capable of becoming something more.

But that was not the end of my story. I found God. Through faith, hard work, accountability and the people who helped me keep moving, I began rebuilding my life piece by piece.

That struggle gave me a purpose bigger than simply surviving it. Broken Growth Ministries exists because I want to give back. I want people facing addiction, homelessness, incarceration and reentry to have access to housing, work, recovery support, mentorship, faith and a community willing to stand beside them while they rebuild.

I cannot change somebody's past. But I can help make sure their past does not have to determine their future.
