Website privacy

Privacy information

This website is a public information and outreach site for Broken Growth Ministries.

Information collected by this site

The website does not currently operate its own account system, payment processor, newsletter database or contact-form database. Contact links open your email application, and donation links send you to PayPal.

Campaign and interaction data

The site contains privacy-conscious event hooks for items such as donation-button clicks, partner links and page scroll depth. These hooks do not themselves send data to an analytics provider. If analytics is enabled later, this notice should be updated before collecting data beyond what is necessary for ordinary website operation.

Third-party services

GitHub Pages hosts this website. PayPal handles donations made through the PayPal link. Email interactions are handled by your email provider and the ministry's email provider. Those services operate under their own privacy terms.

Questions about this website can be sent to BrokenGrowthMinistry7@proton.me.
