Our mission

A pathway—not a handout.

Broken Growth Ministries is building a practical reentry and restoration pathway connecting housing, employment, mentorship, recovery support, life skills, transportation and community relationships.

What we are building

Housing

Transitional tiny-home housing designed to replace instability with structure, dignity and a place to rebuild.

Employment

Job connections, skills development and advancement pathways supporting long-term independence.

Whole-person support

Mentorship, life skills, recovery support, resource navigation and dependable community.

Pre-release connection

Relationship-building before release so reentry planning begins before the first day back in the community.

Why support now

Help build the next chapter.

Support can help develop housing, transportation, training, participant care and the operating capacity needed to launch and sustain the ministry responsibly.
